## Takeaways

- **Advertising** is 49% of unique leads and 41% of closed revenue on $159K spend (2.74x ROAS). **Organic**
  (mostly the Charleston and Myrtle Beach GBP listings plus Google Organic) brings in 1,288 leads and $368K
  with no media cost.
- **Branded search is the most efficient paid line:** PPC_Brand_Core (Zip-Code + Myrtle Beach) spent $7.0K
  and returned $96.8K closed revenue (13.8x).
- **The LSA listings carry paid volume:** the two Coastal Air Plus LSA campaigns spent $34.0K and returned
  $154.4K (4.5x), with 503 leads and 193 booked jobs. Plumbing LSA is the standout at 11.3x.
- **Myrtle Beach non-brand PPC is the weakest:** HVAC, Plumbing, Competitor and Carolina-Cool Myrtle Beach
  spent $54.0K together for $40.6K closed revenue (0.75x). The HVAC and Plumbing campaigns alone spent
  $34.8K for $12.5K.
- **Four offer campaigns produced no closed revenue yet:** August $100 Off HVAC Repair, July–Sept $99 New
  System, June Offers and PDM Sept Plumbing spent $9.3K for 37 leads and 2 booked jobs.
- **Charleston GBP** is the largest single lead source (707 unique leads) but books at a much lower rate
  (74 jobs) than Myrtle Beach GBP (297 leads, 71 jobs).

## Notes

- **Unique leads are summed by month.** The API caps each request at 90 days, so the data was pulled
  with `interval=month` and added up. Someone who became a lead in two different months counts twice,
  so the YTD unique-lead total may run slightly higher than the on-screen report.
- **Unattributed spend:** $9,316.38 of Advertising spend is recorded at the channel level but is not tied
  to any named campaign (typically renamed or removed ad-platform campaigns). It is shown as its own row so
  the Advertising campaign table adds up to the channel total. Leads, booked jobs and revenue reconcile exactly.
- `(no campaign)` rows are leads Searchlight attributed to a channel without a campaign name.
- This uses an **exclude** filter (all campaigns except 45), so it is broader than the older
  `coastal-air-plus-ytd-summary.md`, which used a 43-campaign include list through Sep 15.
